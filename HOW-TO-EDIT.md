# How to update the Bawngkawn Corps website

You never need to touch the website itself. Everything is done in Google Drive.
Changes appear on the website **within about 5 minutes**.

---

## Putting up photos from a programme

1. Open the **Website Photos** folder in Google Drive (on your phone, use the Drive app).
2. Make a **new folder** for the programme. Start the name with the date:

   `2026-10-11 Harvest Thanksgiving`
   `11-10-2026 Harvest Thanksgiving` ← this works too

3. Upload the photos into that folder. Done!

**Good to know**
- Any phone photo works, including iPhone photos.
- Photos appear on the website in the same order as in the folder.
- **To remove a photo** from the website, delete it from the folder.
- **To remove a whole album**, delete the folder.
- **To fix an album's name or date**, rename the folder. (Links people already shared to the old name will stop working.)
- Please don't upload photos someone has asked not to be shared, especially of children.

---

## Putting up a sermon

1. Upload the video to YouTube as usual.
2. Open the website, scroll to the bottom and press **Admin** (or go to `/admin`). Sign in with your name and password.
3. In **Sermons**, paste the YouTube link. The title, thumbnail and date fill in by themselves.
4. Add the preacher, scripture, series, language, a short summary and any notes, then press **Publish**. It is on the website straight away.

**Good to know**
- **Series:** make one in the **Series** tab first, then pick it on the sermon. Renaming a series renames it everywhere.
- **Pin** a sermon to feature it at the top of the home page. With nothing pinned, the newest sermon is shown.
- **Hide** takes a sermon off the website without deleting it. **Delete** can't be undone.
- You stay signed in for 12 hours on that device. Press **Sign out** when you finish on a shared phone or computer.
- Keep passwords private. If someone wrong tries guessing, sign-in locks for everyone for 15 minutes. Wait and try again.

## Choosing which photos show

In **Admin → Photos** you can switch to a different Drive folder, press **Check Drive now** to bring in new photos straight away, and **Hide** an album from the website without deleting it from Drive.

---

## Changing words on the website

Open the Google Sheet called **Website**. Each tab at the bottom is one part of the site.
Change the text in the cells. There's no "save" button; Google saves by itself.

| Tab | What it controls | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Officers** | Corps Officers on the About page | Rank changes: change *Captain* to *Major* |
| **Meetings** | Weekly meeting times | Change *10:00 AM* to *9:30 AM* |
| **Contact** | Phone, email, WhatsApp, address, Facebook/YouTube links | Paste your YouTube channel link (like `https://www.youtube.com/@yourchannel`) for the footer icon |
| **Sections** | The Sections page | |
| **Missions** | The Missions page and the home page | One row per mission. *Album* is optional: type part of a photo album's name and that album's first photo is used as the picture |
| **Give** | The Give page | UPI ID, bank details. Leave a row empty and it just isn't shown |

**Rules to keep it working**
- **Don't change the first row** (the bold headings), and don't rename the tabs.
- **WhatsApp:** just type the number, e.g. `+91 98620 00000`.
- Made a mistake? **File → Version history** lets you go back.

## Counselling requests

When someone fills in the **Talk to someone** form, their message is added as a new row in a **separate, private** Google Sheet (not the Website sheet).

- **Someone must check it every day** and reach out to people kindly and privately. If nobody can promise that, ask the person looking after the website to switch the form off.
- Only the counsellors should have access to that Sheet. Please never share it more widely.
- The responsible person can get an email each time a request arrives (the website's developer sets this up, see `setup/SETUP.md`). The email only says a request came in. The message itself is only in the Sheet.
- The page also shows the emergency number (112) and the Tele-MANAS helpline (14416) for people who need help right now.

## If something looks wrong

- **Waited 10 minutes and nothing changed?** Refresh the page. Check the folder or sheet is the right one.
- **A photo is missing?** It may still be processing if many were uploaded at once. Give it 15 minutes.
- **Still stuck?** Contact: ____________ (fill in who looks after the website)
